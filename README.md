This study presents a cross-organ fibroblast atlas of fibrotic diseases, integrating public single-cell and single-nucleus RNA-sequencing datasets comprising 4,755,536 cells and nuclei from the lung, liver, heart, kidney, and skin. The atlas characterizes five fibroblast subtypes: ACSM3-positive myofibroblasts, inflammatory fibroblasts, matrix fibroblasts, ACTA2-positive myofibroblasts, and antigen-presenting fibroblasts. Transcriptomic analyses describe subtype-associated molecular programs, inferred trajectory relationships, and predicted cell–cell interactions. Complementary chromatin accessibility, spatial transcriptomic, and clinical analyses in lung, heart, and kidney cohorts examine MYC-associated inflammatory programs, tissue-specific spatial associations, and organ-dependent clinical associations. The work provides a reference for investigating fibroblast heterogeneity and candidate regulatory relationships in fibrotic diseases.
